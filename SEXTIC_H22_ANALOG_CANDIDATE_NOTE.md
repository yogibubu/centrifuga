# First Sextic Candidate Beyond the Standard Linear Sector

The standard sextic geometry sector currently implemented in
`compare_gaussian_sextic.py` splits into two parts:

- a part generated by the quartic Wilson tensor `tau`,
- a part generated by the first-level Coriolis-coupled object `c2`.

More precisely,

`Phi_geometry = G_tau(tau) + G_c2(c2),`

where:

- `G_tau` is quadratic in the quartic tensor `tau`,
- `G_c2` depends only on `c2`, which is already generated from `mu1`.

This identifies the natural first post-standard sextic candidate. If one writes

`tau = tau_std + delta_tau,`

then the first sextic correction induced by the non-standard quartic insertion is
the cross term

`G_lin(tau_std; delta_tau) = G_tau(tau_std + delta_tau) - G_tau(tau_std) - G_tau(delta_tau).`

This is the sextic analogue of the quartic `H22` logic:

- `tau_std` stays inside the standard linear sector,
- `delta_tau` is the first non-standard quartic insertion,
- `G_lin` is the first sextic term linear in that insertion,
- `G_tau(delta_tau)` is only the quadratic self-coupling of the new quartic piece.

## Practical consequence

The candidate analogue of `H22` on the sextic side is therefore **not** a new
standard-style block at the level of `c1` or `c2`. It is the first sextic term
linear in a non-standard quartic correction through the `tau`-dependent geometry
functional.

## Technical caveat

The sextic formulas use the **full Wilson quartic tensor** `tau(i,j,k,l)`,
including components of type `iiij`, `ijik`, and `iijk`. These are not
contained in the compressed Watson quartic constants alone.

Therefore, to evaluate the sextic correction induced by `H22`, one needs a
full quartic representative for the `H22` contribution, not just the compressed
quartic constants.

This lift is now implemented directly from the H22 mode-pair contraction. Two
checks are important:

- the full Wilson lift of `H12H12`, after the global conversion
  `4 * AU_FREQ_TO_CMINV`, reproduces the standard sextic-side quartic tensor;
- the full Wilson lift of `H22` projects back exactly to the already existing
  compressed `H22` quartic channel.

So the first sextic candidate induced by `H22` is now numerically available as
the linear response of `G_tau`. On the local `H2O` benchmark, its largest
component is currently the `aaa` entry, of order `1.3e3 Hz`, while the other
components are smaller.
