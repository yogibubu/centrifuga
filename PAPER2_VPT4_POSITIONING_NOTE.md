# Paper 2 Positioning Note

The current project split should now be regarded as fixed.

## Paper 1

Paper 1 isolates the **linear tensor sector** of centrifugal distortion and
its **first breakdown**.

Its scope is:

- standard quartic centrifugal distortion,
- standard sextic centrifugal distortion,
- pseudoinverse gauge fixing of the inverse problem,
- tensor transport between representations and reductions,
- identification of the first breakdown diagnostic through `H22`,
- first sextic analogue through the term linear in `tau(H22)`.

The key conceptual result of Paper 1 is that **standard quartic and standard
sextic centrifugal distortion constants belong to the same linear tensor
sector**.

## Paper 2

Paper 2 is the **full VPT4 problem** and should be framed as the study of the
**nonlinear tensor structure beyond the linear sector**.

Its scope is:

- full quartic VPT4 contributions,
- identification and classification of the tensor objects beyond the
  first-level sector,
- roles of `mu1`, `mu2`, `phi3`, and related higher tensorial ingredients,
- organization of the channels that leave the linear transport sector,
- general tensor hierarchy of centrifugal distortion constants beyond the
  standard quartic/sextic regime.

In short:

- **Paper 1**: linear tensor sector + first breakdown
- **Paper 2**: full VPT4 = nonlinear tensor hierarchy beyond the linear sector

This distinction should be preserved in future drafting so that Paper 1 is not
pulled toward a premature full-VPT4 treatment and Paper 2 can be developed as
the natural continuation.
